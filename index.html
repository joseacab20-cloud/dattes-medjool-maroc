Origin & terroir

Moroccan Medjool dates from the oases of Tafilalet

FAQ – Medjool Dates Wholesale

What is the difference between Medjool and Mejhoul dates?

They are the same variety. "Mejhoul" is the original Moroccan name and "Medjool" is the spelling most used internationally. It is known for its large size, soft flesh and caramel taste.

Where do your wholesale Medjool dates come from?

Our dates come from Morocco, from the Errachidia – Tafilalet region in the south-east of the country, the historic home of the Mejhoul date.

Do you sell Medjool dates wholesale?

Yes. We supply bulk Medjool dates to importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers and food-service buyers, and we also handle smaller orders. Tell us the quantity and destination on WhatsApp to receive a quote.

Which sizes and packaging are available for bulk orders?

Sizes and formats (bulk, cartons, boxes, gift sets) depend on the harvest and on your needs. Message us on WhatsApp for current availability of wholesale Medjool dates.

Can you ship wholesale Medjool dates to the US and other countries?

Export requests, including to the United States, are reviewed case by case. Terms (documents, shipping, lead times) depend on the destination country: contact us on WhatsApp to discuss.

How do I get a wholesale price for Medjool dates?

Send us a WhatsApp message with the product, size, quantity and delivery city or country. We will reply with a personalised wholesale quote.

How should Medjool dates be stored?

Keep them in a cool, dry place away from light, in a closed container. In the fridge they stay soft for longer.
